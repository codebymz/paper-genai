📚 PaperGenAI ke Bare Mein

PaperGenAI ek free AI-powered tool hai jo Punjab Board (PCTB) syllabus ke mutabiq Class 9, 10, 11, aur 12 ke exam papers generate karta hai.

Hamara mission hai ke Pakistan ke har student ko — chahe woh kisi bhi economic background se ho — best quality practice papers access kar sake. Koi signup nahi, koi fees nahi.

Features:

  • ✅ AI Paper Generator (MCQs, Short, Long, Full Paper)
  • ✅ Study Chatbot (Urdu, English, Roman Urdu)
  • ✅ MCQ Quiz Practice
  • ✅ All 9 Punjab BISE Boards + FBISE support
  • ✅ PDF Download & Print

📝 Study Tips & Blog

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

📌 PaperGenAI kya hai aur yeh kaise kaam karta hai?
PaperGenAI ek free AI-powered tool hai jo Punjab Board (PCTB) syllabus ke mutabiq Class 9-12 ke exam papers generate karta hai. Aap class, subject, board, aur paper type select karte hain, phir AI automatically MCQs, short questions, aur long questions ke saath ek authentic board-style paper bana deta hai.
💰 Kya PaperGenAI use karna bilkul free hai?
Haan! PaperGenAI bilkul free hai. Koi signup nahi, koi fees nahi, koi hidden charges nahi. Aap unlimited papers generate kar sakte hain bina kisi payment ke.
📚 Kin classes ke liye papers bana sakta hoon?
Aap Class 9, 10, 11 (1st Year), aur 12 (2nd Year) ke liye papers bana sakte hain. PCTB syllabus ke mutabiq sabhi subjects available hain.
🏫 Kin boards ke liye support hai?
Hum 9 Punjab BISE boards (Lahore, Faisalabad, Sahiwal, Multan, Gujranwala, Rawalpindi, DG Khan, Sargodha, Bahawalpur) aur FBISE (Federal Board) ko support karte hain.
📝 Kaise paper generate karein?
Home page se "Paper Generator" select karein, phir class, subject, board, paper type choose karein, chapters select karein, aur "Generate" button dabayein. AI automatic paper bana de ga jise aap print ya PDF download kar sakte hain.
🤖 Study Chatbot kaise istemal karein?
Chatbot panel mein apni class aur subject select karein, phir "Chat Shuru Karo" dabayein. Iske baad aap koi bhi sawal puch sakte hain — chapter summary, definitions, MCQs practice, ya short questions — AI Urdu ya English mein jawab de ga.
🎯 MCQ Quiz kaise khelein?
Quiz panel mein class aur subject choose karein, questions ki quantity select karein (5, 10, 15, 20), phir "Quiz Start Karo" dabayein. MCQs attempt karein aur natija dekhein. Behtareen score pe confetti bhi aata hai!
📄 Kya generated paper ko PDF mein download kar sakta hoon?
Haan! Paper generate hone ke baad "PDF" button par click karein. Aap "Print" bhi kar sakte hain. Paper A4 format mein white page par authentic board-style mein display hota hai.
🌙 Day/Night theme kaise change karein?
Settings panel mein "Dark Mode" toggle par click karein. Day theme ke liye light mode aur night theme ke liye dark mode select karein. Theme smooth crossfade ke saath change hota hai.
📱 Kya yeh mobile par bhi kaam karta hai?
Bilkul! PaperGenAI fully responsive hai aur mobile, tablet, aur desktop sab par behtareen kaam karta hai. Bottom dock navigation mobile-friendly hai.
